The patient experience

A more thoughtful treatment journey.

Good care starts with understanding. The right treatment pathway should reflect your concerns, medical history and individual suitability.

Understand

Begin by discussing your skin concerns, goals and relevant medical history with the clinic.

Assess

A professional assessment helps determine which treatment options may be suitable for your needs.

Plan

Understand the proposed approach, possible alternatives and the expectations discussed with your specialist.

Prepare

Receive relevant preparation and aftercare guidance before proceeding with a treatment.

Follow Up

Where appropriate, review progress and discuss any adjustments to the treatment plan with your medical team.

Maintain

Discuss suitable ongoing care, skin protection and maintenance recommendations for your individual needs.

Ask the Clinic
What treatments does Dr. Faiez Ghanam Clinic offer?

The clinic provides medical dermatology, cosmetic procedures and laser services. Published treatment information includes vitiligo care, acne treatment, pigmentation procedures, hair-related services and aesthetic treatments.

How do I know which treatment is suitable for me?

Treatment suitability depends on your skin condition, medical history, goals and a professional assessment. Contact the clinic to discuss arranging a consultation.

Does the clinic provide vitiligo treatment?

Yes. The clinic publishes information about vitiligo management, including light-based and topical treatment approaches. A specialist should assess your individual circumstances and discuss suitable options.

How can I book an appointment?

You can contact the clinic by telephone at +971 4 349 3555 or send an appointment enquiry through its WhatsApp contact.

Where is the clinic located?

The clinic is located at Villa #6, 122 Jumeirah Beach Road, facing the Old Dubai Zoo, Jumeirah First, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

What should I expect during a consultation?

A consultation is an opportunity to explain your concerns, discuss relevant medical history and understand potential treatment options. The practitioner can advise on suitability, risks, preparation and aftercare.
