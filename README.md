# Dr. Faiez Ghanam Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Dr. Faiez Ghanam Dermatology, Cosmetic & Laser Center is a Dubai-based specialist clinic providing medical dermatology, aesthetic medicine and laser treatments. Led by Dr. Faiez Ghanam, who has over 30 years of experience, the clinic serves patients seeking professional skin care, cosmetic procedures and personalised treatment programmes.

The redesigned homepage focuses on establishing medical credibility, presenting treatments around patient concerns and creating a clearer, more professional experience for prospective patients.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Specialist Expertise and Medical Credibility

- Prioritises Dr. Faiez Ghanam's 30-plus years of dermatology and cosmetic medicine experience.
- Gives greater visibility to the clinic's specialist expertise, particularly vitiligo treatment.
- Replaces excessive luxury-focused messaging with clearer doctor-led communication.

**Issue addressed:** The original homepage relied heavily on promotional language without sufficiently highlighting the clinic's medical expertise and specialist credentials.

### 2. More Patient-Focused Treatment Navigation

- Organises treatment discovery around common skin conditions and patient concerns.
- Makes services such as acne, pigmentation, vitiligo, hair loss and skin rejuvenation easier to identify.
- Creates clearer pathways towards relevant treatment information and consultation enquiries.

**Issue addressed:** The original navigation organised treatments mainly by categories such as Face, For Men, For Women and Body, making it harder for patients to find services based on their specific concerns.

### 3. Clearer and More Consistent Clinic Information

- Highlights essential clinic contact information more clearly.
- Identifies the need to verify the correct contact email and operating hours.
- Establishes consistency as a priority across the website, appointment journey and official business listings.

**Issue addressed:** The original website and Dubai Health Authority directory displayed conflicting email addresses and operating hours, potentially creating confusion for prospective patients.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Establishes a more professional healthcare-focused appearance that supports the clinic's specialist positioning.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Gives greater prominence to the lead doctor's expertise, key treatments and patient-focused information.
- **Navigation:** Improves treatment discoverability by connecting services with common patient concerns.
- **User Experience:** Creates clearer journeys from treatment discovery to consultation enquiries.
- **Content Consistency:** Prioritises accurate clinic information and consistent communication across important website sections.
- **Overall Direction:** Balances the clinic's established aesthetic positioning with a stronger emphasis on medical expertise, patient reassurance and accessible treatment information.

---

**Project Focus:** Medical credibility, patient-focused treatment discovery and consistent clinic information.
